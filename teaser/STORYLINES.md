# Storylines for the Space Demo teaser

About 30 seconds, 1920x1080, YouTube and the readme. No voiceover. All footage is recorded from the running Demo.

The one thing: "One Ship, alone in a cold System, and it is yours."

## A. One Warp (recommended)

- **Device and form:** the result first, as one continuous journey. The whole film is a single trip from The Scatter to Lantern Station.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** A small Ship with a warm engine sits among dark rocks. It turns, the stars stretch, and it arrives in front of a lit station that says nothing back. Lonely first, then a rush, then quiet company.
- **First two seconds:** the Ship in close-up, engine glowing amber, rocks drifting behind it. The line `NOBODY IS EXPECTING YOU` types on in HUD type.
- **Beats:** Ship among rocks → Overview row clicked, `[ WARP TO LANTERN STATION ]` → aligning → tunnel → arrival, station dead ahead → line `THE UNIVERSE IS YOURS` → end card with the name and repo address.
- **Look:** the Demo's own black, cold blue light and amber HUD monospace. Titles are set as if the HUD printed them.
- **Sound:** the repo's CC0 ambient pad throughout, one rising sweep on the Warp, a soft tick on each click.

## B. Four places

- **Device and form:** a flight through one world, cut on the music. Four stops, each a few seconds, each named by its Overview row.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** The Scatter, Lantern Station, Morrow and the star Tarn, one after another, joined by short Warp flashes. It shows how much is in the System; it feels like a tour more than a journey.
- **First two seconds:** Morrow filling the sky with the Ship tiny against it, and the Overview row `MORROW  4.2 AU` highlighted.
- **Beats:** Morrow → Warp flash → The Scatter → Warp flash → Lantern Station → Warp flash → Tarn, brightest shot → line `ONE SYSTEM. FOUR PLACES. NO ONE ELSE.` → end card.
- **Look:** as A, with each place's name set large in amber over its shot.
- **Sound:** the ambient pad, a short sweep on each Warp flash.

## C. The Unknown (the bold one)

- **Device and form:** a moment in someone's day, held as one frame that rebuilds. The camera stays on the Overview and the Ship; the story is a Contact that appears and never answers.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** A quiet Ship. A row reading `UNKNOWN` appears on the Overview. The viewer waits for something to happen. It leaves. The Ship Warps away alone. Eerie and memorable, but it leans on a Contact turning up on cue.
- **First two seconds:** the Overview in close-up; a new row `UNKNOWN` ticks into the list.
- **Beats:** Unknown appears → Ship selects it, bracket on empty space → no Warp button, no reply → the row vanishes → line `SOMETIMES THERE IS SOMEONE` → Ship Warps → line `MOSTLY THERE IS YOU` → end card.
- **Look:** as A, tighter on the HUD, long holds.
- **Sound:** the ambient pad very low, one tick when the Unknown appears, near silence when it leaves.
