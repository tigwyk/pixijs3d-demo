# Space Demo

A single-player mood piece: one ship alone in one star system. It exists to make the player feel cold emptiness and cozy excitement at once, not to be a game.

## Language

**Demo**:
The whole experience: a goal-less, fail-less mood piece meant to be sat in for a couple of minutes.
_Avoid_: Game, level, mission

**Ship**:
The single vessel the player commands and the camera orbits. It is the only source of warmth in the scene.
_Avoid_: Player, avatar, craft

**Command**:
An instruction the player gives the Ship. There are exactly three: set a Heading, Stop, and Warp to a Celestial. The player never steers directly.
_Avoid_: Control, input, flying

**Heading**:
The direction the Ship has been commanded to travel in, set by double-clicking in space.
_Avoid_: Course, direction, waypoint

**System**:
The one star system the Demo takes place in; the entire extent of the universe.
_Avoid_: Universe, world, map, level

**Celestial**:
A fixed destination in the System (star, planet, station or wreck, asteroid field) that the Ship can Warp to.
_Avoid_: Location, POI, node

**Grid**:
The local space around one Celestial, in which the Ship moves under its own power. Grids are not connected; only Warp moves the Ship between them.
_Avoid_: Scene, zone, area, sector

**Warp**:
The only way to travel between Grids: a scripted align, tunnel and arrival sequence. It is the Demo's "exciting" beat.
_Avoid_: Jump, teleport, travel

**Backdrop**:
The infinitely distant sky seen from every Grid: stars, nebula, and far-off impostors of the star and planets.
_Avoid_: Skybox, background, sky

**Contact**:
An unnamed sign of another presence in the System, shown on the Overview at unreachable distance. It cannot be warped to and never resolves into an interaction.
_Avoid_: NPC, enemy, lifeform, other ship

**Overview**:
The HUD list of Celestials and Contacts with their distances.
_Avoid_: Radar, minimap, target list
